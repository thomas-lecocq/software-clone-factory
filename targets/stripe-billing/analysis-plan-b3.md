# Analysis plan: stripe-billing, holdout batch 3

Pre-registered on 2026-10-13 and committed before any fixture of the batch is recorded (rule 3 of
`docs/verification.md`). Nothing below may change once the first `hold-b3-` fixture exists; a
change means a new batch with a new seed.

## The batch

- **Probes:** `hold-b3-001` to `hold-b3-473`, generated by `wideProbesV2` in `probes.ts` from the
  `hold-b3` seed in `holdout-seed.json`, drawn when this plan was written. Every `hold-` probe is
  holdout by construction (`splitOf`); the seed file never enters a builder sandbox.
- **Distribution:** `WIDE_DISTRIBUTION_V2` in `probes.ts`: the slice 1 change of batches 1 and 2,
  widened where breaker round 1 found the clone failing (up to 4 items, amounts from 50 cents to
  $100,000 on a log scale, percent coupons up to 100 %, amount-off coupons up to $10,000,
  quantity 0, interval changes on any item count) and drawing fewer easy cases (the `none`
  behaviour 5 % instead of 15 %; most changes that would change nothing become quantity changes).
  The builder may train on the 300 `wide3-` probes drawn from it (their dev share).
- **Recording:** once, with `npm run record`, Stripe test mode, each probe twice for noise.
- **Size:** 473, as for batch 2: below 1 % at 99 % with no failure, below 1 % at 95 % with one.

## The claim and the decision rule

- **Claim under test:** on `WIDE_DISTRIBUTION_V2`, the clone's per-case divergence rate is below
  1 %.
- **A case passes** when `npm run verify` finds no blocking difference under `policy.json` as
  locked at the measurement commit. A crash counts as a failure. Any change to `policy.json`
  between this plan and the measurement is listed in the fidelity report.
- **Decision**, with k failures out of 473 and one-sided exact (Clopper-Pearson) upper bounds:
  - k = 0: "below 1 % at 99 % confidence" (bound 0.97 %);
  - k = 1: "below 1 % at 95 % confidence" (bound 0.999 %), not at 99 %;
  - k ≥ 2: the claim is not supported; report "k of 473" and both bounds.
- **Reported apart, no claim:** the share of easy cases (Stripe errors, changes that change
  nothing) and the result on the others.

## The measurement

- **Measured clone:** the clone measured on batch 2 (`claim-batch-2`), after one more builder pass
  in the agent container that may use every dev fixture (the breaker's, the mutation-testing
  probes, `wide3-`) and never sees batch 3. The clone is frozen at a commit before batch 3 is
  scored.
- **One look:** `npm run verify stripe-billing -- --split holdout` runs once on that commit. Its
  line in `verify/runs.jsonl` is the result; the fidelity report checks that this plan was
  committed before the batch. A run that stops before scoring may be repeated; a scored run
  counts.
- **Afterwards the batch is spent.** The result goes into `measurements.md`, whatever it is.
